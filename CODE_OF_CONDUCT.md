# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1)

Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected Behavior

Be kind and respectful. Provide constructive feedback, be mindful of differing perspectives, and avoid demeaning or exclusionary language.

Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language is not tolerated. Repeated or severe violations will be addressed by the maintainers.

Reporting

If you are subject to or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or contact the repository maintainers. Please include as much information as possible.

Enforcement

Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and may take actions including warnings, removal of contributions, or banning, depending on severity.

Attribution
